# 2026-10-09 — S16: reworking the R-checks

Companion to `memory-bank/activeContext.md`. Durable rules live in `docs/lessons.md`;
the step's own record is `docs/todo.md` and `docs/reviews/S16-findings.md`.

## What the step was
`make verify` had to reach 17 PASS again, testing the same properties against infrastructure
that S15 had moved out of the cluster. `app/scripts/verify.sh` read Redis and Postgres with
`kubectl exec` into pods that no longer existed.

## The environment change
`default` still ran the Stage A app (redis Deployment, postgres StatefulSet + PVC, its own
Service and Secret). S16.sh's precondition is a migrated namespace, so the pre-migration
objects were deleted there and `app/kustomize` applied; the controller then provisioned
`default-redis-redis`, `default-postgres-postgres` and `default-pgadmin-pgadmin`, wrote
`jit-redis` / `jit-postgres` / `jit-pgadmin` and the three Deployments reached Ready. The
old in-cluster tally was lost with the PVC — its `psql` read returned nothing even before
teardown, consistent with the old `votingdb` mismatch that the module's `POSTGRES_DB=voting`
closed. That deploy is also what made `S00.sh` fail honestly for the first time since S15.

## What "the six checks" turned out to mean
The build plan named R2, R8, R9, R10, R11, R16, R17 (seven rows, called six). Also broken:
R3, R4, R6 (they read the tiers through `psql_q`) and R7 (it called `kubectl exec "$PGPOD"`
directly). Rewriting the two helpers to `docker exec` fixed R3/R4/R6 without touching them.
Real split: eleven invalidated, six unaffected (R1, R5, R12-R15). R14's cluster-wide Service
scan and R15's unscoped pod list are the residual `default` assumptions — S17's.

## The review — CONCERNS, no blockers (e17afc7)
1. The helpers return the empty string on failure, so two failed reads compare equal: R3
   could PASS on `"" == ""`. Recommendation was for S17's `verify-jit.sh` to guard or use a
   sentinel.
2. `build-plan.md`'s "Eleven checks are unaffected. These six are not" was stale on both
   numbers.

Both were fixed in this step (3cdd4b6) rather than deferred, and the decision is logged in
`docs/lessons.md` per the runbook's CONCERNS path. The S16 review box stays the human's.

## The defect the fix introduced, and how it surfaced
Guarding R8 with an early `fail` skipped R8's scale-back-to-1, which lived inside the branch
it bypassed — the worker was left at **0 replicas** with nothing consuming the queue. The
negative test caught it, the checkpoint could not: `verify.sh` run with
`REDIS_CONTAINER`/`POSTGRES_CONTAINER` pointed at non-existent containers printed
`worker desired=0` afterwards. Moving the restore outside the branches fixed it; the re-run
ends `worker desired=1 ready=1`. A pause-and-observe check's early exits are part of the
change, not just its comparison.
